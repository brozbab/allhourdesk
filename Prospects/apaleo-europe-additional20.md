- **No individual LinkedIn profiles have been verified.** Do not treat a name-only LinkedIn search result as a confirmed decision-maker.
- Prioritize confirmed email/phone contacts for initial outreach: Eklo, Nestor, Zetter, Hiisi, Hotel-B, Made in Louise. Others require contact-page and role research.
- **Competitive note:** Nestor already reports using AI guest communication, so lead with differentiating voice booking functionality rather than generic AI messaging.
- Validate current PMS, hotel footprint, inbound call volumes, language needs, existing voice AI, booking integration permissions, and procurement owner before advancing to a pilot.
