## Research and quality rules

1. Require **two real, named people per operator**, with current business role and individually attributed professional contact details from primary sources. Generic info@, reservations@, or guessed email patterns do not qualify.
2. Prefer a dated, named Apaleo case study or Apaleo customer listing, with link. Label historic customer evidence separately from current PMS confirmation.
3. Record source URLs for each person; flag outdated roles, ambiguous identities and unverified LinkedIn pages.
4. Do not represent an operator as an individual hotel; track property count and locations separately.
5. Never inflate the list to hit 40. Count only verified, contactable entries.
6. Follow applicable B2B marketing and privacy laws.
