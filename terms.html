3. What the service does

AllHourDesk answers telephone calls made to a hotel's line, converses with callers using an AI voice agent, queries availability in the hotel's connected systems, places temporary holds, issues payment links, and confirms or cancels reservations — as configured by and on the instructions of that hotel.

The agent identifies itself as an AI at the start of every call.
