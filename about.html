About us

Hotels don't need another chatbot.

They need the phone answered at 3am by something that can actually finish the job — check real availability, hold the room, take the money, and put a confirmed booking in the system. That gap is the whole reason this company exists.

01

Why we started

Every independent hotel already has a number guests know, and almost none of them can afford to staff it around the clock.

The calls that fall through aren't marginal ones. A guest phoning a hotel at midnight is usually phoning because they need a room tonight — the most booking-ready caller a property will ever get. The existing options are an answering machine, an overflow call centre reading from a script with no access to inventory, or nothing at all. All three end the same way: the guest books somewhere that picked up.

Voice AI made the first half of this solvable a while ago. The half nobody had done properly was the transaction — reliably holding real inventory, collecting payment out-of-band, and confirming or unwinding the booking without ever double-selling a room. That's the problem we chose to build around.

02

How we build

The back-end is a plug-in

Hotels run wildly different systems and we don't get to pick. Every one of them sits behind a single internal interface, so supporting a new PMS never means rebuilding the product around it.

Money and inventory get the paranoid treatment

Anything touching a payment or a room block is idempotent, logged, and has an explicit compensating action. The "paid but not booked" case is designed for, not discovered in production.

Compliance is an input

AI disclosure, recording consent, PCI scope and data residency shaped the architecture from the first design document, rather than being audited on afterwards.

Say the failure out loud

When a lookup fails, the agent tells the caller there's a technical problem instead of inventing a plausible rate. We'd rather lose a booking than create one that isn't real.
