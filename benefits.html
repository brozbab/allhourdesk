Benefits

What actually changes at the property.

No projected revenue figures on this page. The honest version is narrower and more useful: here is the specific work that stops falling on your team, and the specific failure modes that stop being possible.

01

For the owner or GM

The 3am call gets answered

A guest whose flight was cancelled is calling every hotel near the airport. Whoever answers gets the booking. Out of hours, that is currently nobody.

Direct, not through an OTA

A call that converts on your own line is a direct booking at your own rate, with no channel commission attached to it.

No overnight staffing decision

Cover the night without hiring for it, and without asking a duty manager to sleep next to a phone.

You can read every call

Transcripts and a full booking trail, so "what happened on that reservation" is a lookup rather than an argument.

02

For the front desk

The phone stops winning

With forward-on-no-answer, your line rings first and the guest in front of you keeps your attention. Only the calls you can't reach fall through.

No message backlog

Nothing to call back in the morning, because the booking was completed on the call rather than written on a pad.

The repetitive questions

Check-in time, parking, pets, breakfast, late arrival — answered identically every time, without pulling anyone off the desk.

Nothing new to learn

It sits behind your existing number and writes into your existing PMS. There is no second system for staff to keep in sync.

03

Failure modes that stop being possible

This is the part that took the most engineering, and it's the part most worth asking any vendor about.

NO DOUBLEA room being quoted to a caller is held in your back-end before payment is requested, so it cannot be sold underneath them mid-call.
NO GHOSTA hold that is never paid for releases itself on a deadline. Unpaid rooms don't sit blocked until someone notices.
NO LIMBOIf payment succeeds but the booking can't be committed, the money is refunded or the case is escalated to your team. It is never silently kept.
NO INVENTIONRates and availability come from tool calls. If the lookup fails, the agent reports a technical problem instead of quoting a number it made up.
04

The fair objections

"Guests hate robots"

Some do. That's why it's positioned as overflow: your team answers first, and the alternative for the calls that fall through isn't a human — it's ringing out. The agent also says it's an AI up front rather than pretending otherwise.

"I don't want to replace staff"

Forward-on-no-answer is the default precisely because it doesn't. It covers the hours and the overflow you're not staffing today.

"What if it books something wrong"

It can only hold and confirm what your back-end returns, every action is logged, and cancellation with refund is one of the five operations.

"Switching costs"

A pilot is call forwarding on your existing number. Turning it off restores your previous setup the same day, with nothing ported and nothing migrated.
